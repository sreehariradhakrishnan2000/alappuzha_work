Frequently Asked Questions

How many days are sufficient for an Alleppey trip?

To enjoy a night in a houseboat and the scenic wonders of Alleppey, a 2-day holiday is sufficient.

Is the Alleppey houseboat package safe?

Yes, all our crew members are well-trained and certified in first aid and life-saving techniques. We operate under approved safety standards.

What happens if someone falls ill on the houseboat?

Our trained crew will provide first aid and transport the person by boat to the nearest land point for transfer to a hospital.

Is vegetarian food available on the houseboat?

Yes, pure vegetarian food is available. Notify us about dietary restrictions, gluten intolerance, lactose intolerance, or allergies during booking.

How many people can the houseboat accommodate?

We have single-bedroom houseboats accommodating 2 people to large multi-bedroom houseboats accommodating 20-30 people.

What's the best time to book a houseboat in Alappuzha?

The best time is during the winter season, from October to March, with pleasant cool and dry weather.
